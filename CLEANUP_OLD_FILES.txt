# Optional cleanup after applying the patch

These files belong to the retired Mini App, payment/membership, or Nexus Intel surfaces.
They are no longer imported or used by the updated Telegram bot (`render.yaml` starts `python bot.py`).

After making a backup, you may remove these from the full project:
- main.py (old FastAPI/Mini App wrapper)
- miniapp.py
- index.html
- membership.html
- premium_pass_migration.sql (do not run this migration again)
- render-miniapp.yaml
- intel.py
- intel_worker.py
- render-intel-cron.yaml
- static/ (old Mini App static assets; delete only if nothing else in your project uses them)

Database safety:
- The patch does not drop or alter legacy payment/pass tables.
- Do not delete existing PostgreSQL tables or reset Neon.
- Existing question-bank, published-test, attempt, and group-quiz data are retained.
