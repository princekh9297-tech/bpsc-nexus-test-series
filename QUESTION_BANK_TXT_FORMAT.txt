D) Jupiter
Answer: B

RULES
- Use one question per block. Numbering is recommended.
- Use A) through E) for options. Standalone labels (A on one line, option text on the next) are also supported.
- Mark the correct option with ✓, ✔, ☑ or ✅, OR add Answer: B.
- Start explanations with Explanation:, Solution: or Reason:.
- Blank lines between questions are recommended.
- Question, option and explanation text may span multiple lines.
- If no correct answer is detected, the question is imported as pending verification.
- Questions need at least two options to be recognized as MCQs.
